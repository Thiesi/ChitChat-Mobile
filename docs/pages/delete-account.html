Delete your ChitChat account

ChitChat is an app for self-hosted chat servers. Your account doesn't belong to the app maker: it lives on the ChitChat server you signed up with, run by that server's operators.

In the app

Open ChitChat, go to You → Privacy and security → Delete account, and follow the steps. This deletes your account on that server only.

Without the app

Sign in to your server's website and use its account closure option, or contact the people who run your server.

What happens

You're signed out right away. For 14 days you can restore the account by signing in with Restore a closed account. After that, your name is replaced with "Closed account", your personal details are cleared and your username is freed. Messages and files you sent stay visible to the people who received them, under that server's retention policies, as do moderation and security records.

What the app maker has

Nothing to delete. The app talks to the server you chose, and to the notification delivery app you choose if you turn notifications on. It has no analytics or advertising.
